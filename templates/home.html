What is
The Golf Room

At The Golf Room, our focus is on what we can do for you, the client. Step into a space where every swing brings you closer to your golfing goals. The Golf Room is a haven for golf enthusiasts, offering personalised coaching that addresses your specific needs. The Golf Room offers TrackMan technology, a custom putting surface with training zones and video analysis of each golfer during their lessons.

Whether you're looking to refine your swing, perfect your putting, or enhance your overall course management skills, I'm here to guide you every step of the way. To get started on your golfing journey schedule an online consultation. During this session, we'll discuss your goals, assess your current skills, and develop a bespoke lesson programme tailored to your objectives. Choose personalised performance at The Golf Room — where your success is our mission. Book your 1:1 consultation with Eamonn today and take the first step towards achieving your golfing aspirations.

Your Golf Coach

Led by PGA Professional Eamonn O'Flanagan, your bespoke lesson programme is tailored to your individual requirements helping you overcome problems and elevate your game to new heights of excellence. Utilising Trackman data and video analysis, your personalised coaching session is designed to ensure precision and passion converge seamlessly, resulting in tangible improvements for you.
