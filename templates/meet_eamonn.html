Eamonn
O'Flanagan

PGA Professional & Owner
The Golf Room

"I have been a PGA Professional since completing my training in 2002. My golf career has always revolved around coaching players and helping them develop and remain in the game. I have always been motivated to improve my knowledge and educate myself as my career advanced. This has been achieved through formal CPD courses, working alongside fellow PGA Members and completing an MSc in Sport & Exercise Psychology.

My experience has bridged from introducing new golfers to the game and developing their skills, to coaching elite amateurs and aspiring professional golfers. I have an established reputation as a sought after golf coach. Having completed my PGA training in 2002, I joined the team at The Heritage Golf & Spa Resort where I was the teaching professional at the Seve Ballesteros "Natural" Golf School for numerous years and remained at The Heritage until 2014.

Appointed in 2007 by the Leinster Branch of The Golfing Union of Ireland as a provincial coach, I worked with some of the country's most promising golfing talents, including numerous internationals. I have successfully helped pupils win championships at underage and senior level both on the domestic circuit and internationally. I currently work with Golf Ireland's HP Programme coaching players within the performance pathway.

When not actively coaching clients, the majority of my time is focused on developing my teaching model through accredited CPD/conferences and informal discussions with fellow PGA coaches. My coaching career has established me as one of Ireland's most sought-after golf coaches and I look forward to helping you.

Thank you for choosing The Golf Room to elevate your game!"
